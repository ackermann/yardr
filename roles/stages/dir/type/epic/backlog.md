An epic is shaped here: its goal and "done when" are written and its children
added (`yardr dep add <epic> <child> -k parent`, which also makes each child
block the epic). No agent works this stage.

In a directory rig an epic only groups its children, which work in the same
directory; there is no epic branch. The epic leaves for `open` once it has
its children and they cover its goal: `yardr bead advance <epic> --to open`.
The stage has no outcomes. The children are shaped and advanced on their
own, in the rig's flow.
