The whole of an epic is reviewed here: what its children produced in the
rig's directory, as a whole. Each child was reviewed when it was done; the
job here is the sum.

Read the epic and every child (`yardr bead show <child>`; their notes name
the files). Check the whole against the epic's goal and "done when": do the
children's outputs fit together, is anything a child left "for later"
actually done. Fix forward by editing in place, and run the rig's gate if it
has one.

Approval here closes the epic. Before it leaves, one note on it says how the
review ended. Outcomes:

- `done`, approved: the note says what you checked, every file you changed,
  the gate result, and any remaining risk. The epic is closed.
- `changes`: the epic goes back to `open`; the fix is then a new child the
  mayor files, so make the finding concrete enough to be a bead.
- `question`, it needs a decision only {{operator}} can make: the note has
  the question and the options, and the epic goes to `decide`.
