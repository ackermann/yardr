The train waits for a person: Christopher decides. No agent works this stage.
The train carries the question and the options, written by whoever sent it
here; if it does not, ask them before deciding.

The train leaves once the answer is on it, as a note. The stage has no
outcomes; the person answering moves it (`yardr review -g decisions`, or
`yardr bead advance <train> --to <stage>`):

- to `open`, when the train can go on: with no child open it is integrated
  again, and an answer that needs work is a new child, added first;
- to `backlog`, when the answer changes what the train is and the yardmaster must
  reshape it.
