An epic is shaped here: the mayor writes its goal and "done when" and adds its
children (`yardr dep add <epic> <child> -k parent`, which also makes each
child block the epic). No agent works this stage.

The epic leaves for `open` once it has its children and they cover its goal:
`yardr bead advance <epic> --to open`. The stage has no outcomes. The
children are shaped and advanced on their own, in the rig's flow.
